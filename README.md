# Life: Sigmoid + Noise 📈

An interactive visualization exploring the mathematical nature of happiness across a lifetime.

## The Equation

$$\text{Life} = \int_{\text{birth}}^{\text{death}} [\sigma(t) + \text{noise}(t)] \, dt$$

Where:
- **σ(t)** is the sigmoid function representing your overall happiness trajectory
- **noise(t)** captures the real day-to-day fluctuations and unpredictability of life
- The integral represents the total accumulated happiness from birth to death

## The Philosophy

Life isn't constant. Happiness doesn't stay the same across every age and stage. A bag of M&Ms means something different at 10 than it does at 40, which is completely different from 80.

This visualization models life as having:
- **Early years**: Slow climb, building up experiences
- **Peak years**: Steep rise where happiness accelerates—new relationships, achievements, golden moments
- **Later years**: Plateau into wisdom, acceptance, contentment
- **Always**: Random ups and downs—because life fluctuates, it doesn't follow a perfect curve

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Watch the curve animate from birth to death
3. Adjust the parameters to explore different life trajectories:
   - **Steepness (k)**: How quickly you reach peak happiness
   - **Peak Time (%)**: When your prime happiness years occur
   - **Noise Level**: How chaotic/unpredictable your life is
4. Hit **Reset** to restart the animation

## Features

- 🎬 **Smooth Animation**: Watch your life unfold in real-time
- 📊 **Interactive Controls**: Experiment with different life patterns
- 📈 **Dual Curves**: See both the noisy reality and the clean trend
- 🎨 **Beautiful Design**: Modern gradient UI with smooth interactions
- 📐 **Real Math**: Built on actual sigmoid functions and calculus concepts

## Early Bloomer vs Late Bloomer

Want to see someone who peaked early? Lower the Peak Time. Want someone who found their stride later? Increase it. Crank up the noise to see someone whose life was chaotic AF. Dial it down for someone with steady, predictable contentment.

Every life has a story. This is just one way to visualize it.

## Technical Notes

- Built with vanilla **JavaScript** and **Canvas** API
- Uses **MathJax** for beautiful equation rendering
- No dependencies—just HTML, CSS, and JS
- Responsive and works on most modern browsers

## The Real Talk

This equation came from recognizing that the oversimplified formula `Life = (happiness/time) × Δtime` treats happiness as a constant—which is objectively false. Real happiness fluctuates, peaks, plateaus, and changes with age and circumstance.

The sigmoid captures the *shape* of a life. The noise captures the *texture* of living it.
